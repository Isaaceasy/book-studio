# PROFILE: SELF-HELP, BUSINESS AND PERSONAL FINANCE (nonfiction how-to)

Applies to self-help, productivity, business, leadership, personal finance and similar practical nonfiction. All standing rules in `CLAUDE.md` apply. Default output: KDP PDF (color or black and white per the product spec) plus ebook.

## What decides success here
Reader trust and honest promises. This niche is full of income and transformation claims that violate Amazon's rules and disappoint readers. A book wins by being specific, useful and verifiable.

## Structure
Promise-to-payoff chapters: hook (a concrete situation), the idea in plain words, a worked example, an exercise or decision tool, a summary of the takeaway. 8 to 16 chapters. Each chapter has one home concept (non-repetition rule applies). Exercises are real and doable in 10 to 20 minutes. Case examples are fictional composites labelled illustrative; no real people, no fake testimonials.

## Claims and safety audit (class audit for every chapter)
- (X) Income or outcome promise: any statement about earnings, results, timelines or guarantees is removed or qualified.
- (L) Legal, tax or investment advice: general education only, with the disclaimer; no personalised advice; verify every rule, rate and threshold against current official sources for the stated region and date.
- (H) Health or psychological claims: no cure, diagnosis or treatment claims; recommend professional help where relevant.
- (S) Statistics and studies: every number opened and logged; no "studies show" without a source in the log.
- (T) Trademarks and public figures: none in title, subtitle, description, keywords or cover; mention in the manuscript only when essential.
Financial books carry a front-matter disclaimer approved by the owner and state the currency date.

## Length
Set in the Scope Lock (typical 25,000 to 60,000 words). No padding; competitors' lengths come from `cms-research.md`.

## Interior and cover
Clean, airy, color accents; callouts for "Try this", "Remember", "Common mistake"; worksheets as real tables; use library cards from `interiors/` matched to the niche. Cover conventions from CMS (bold type, high contrast, thumbnail legibility).

## Expert review
Optional but recommended for finance and legal topics: a qualified reviewer's written sign-off recorded in `status.md`; no "expert-verified" wording without it.
