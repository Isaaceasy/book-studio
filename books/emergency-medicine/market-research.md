## 2. Unit economics (completed; source: KDP pages above, read 2026-10-28)

Rules read: royalty is 60% of list price at USD 9.99 or more; printing cost = fixed + pages x per-page cost. US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) counts as LARGE trim (wider than 6.12 in). Large trim: black ink, 110 to 828 pages: USD 1.00 + 0.017 per page. Standard color, 72 to 600 pages: USD 1.00 + 0.0402 per page. Premium color, 42 to 828 pages: USD 1.00 + 0.08 per page. Maximum list price USD 250. Print cost varies by marketplace and options.

Page count is an ESTIMATE (12 Parts x ~11,000 words, 12 pt, plus figures and tables): 300, 350 or 400 pages. Royalty = 0.60 x price minus print cost.

| Pages | Print cost B&W | Royalty B&W at $24.99 / $29.99 / $34.99 | Print cost std color | Royalty std color at $24.99 / $29.99 / $34.99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | $6.10 | $8.89 / $11.89 / $14.89 | $13.06 | $1.93 / $4.93 / $7.93 |
| 350 | $6.95 | $8.04 / $11.04 / $14.04 | $15.07 | -$0.08 / $2.92 / $5.92 |
| 400 | $7.80 | $7.19 / $10.19 / $13.19 | $17.08 | -$2.09 / $0.91 / $3.91 |

Premium color (350 pp) costs $29.00 per copy, which is a loss at every price tested. The three candidate prices are NOT observed market prices (price band not seen); they are illustrations only.

Key point: a color interior (the owner's chosen design with crimson accent and 50 figures) cuts royalty by roughly $7 to $10 per copy versus black and white. The color decision must be made deliberately.
