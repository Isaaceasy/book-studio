8. Model answer: (1) Look from a distance with the three-part assessment (how the infant seems, how the infant breathes, the color of the skin) and call for help early if any part is abnormal. (3) Speak calmly to the parent and record the explanation in the parent's own words with the time, without accusing anyone. (4) Report your concern through the channels your service requires, because a bruise in an infant who cannot yet roll over may not fit a fall from a sofa; describe it, report your concern, and leave the judgment to those trained to make it, since you do not need proof to report.
