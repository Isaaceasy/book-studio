## Components
Yellow highlight bars for laws and key rules; colored rule boxes (blue, green, purple); numbered examples; equation blocks; large friendly illustrations of famous scientists or lab scenes with speech-style quotes; mini diagrams in the margin; summary boxes.

## Strengths
Strong subject color coding; yellow highlights make rules instantly findable; illustrations add personality; equations are given room.

## Weaknesses
Highly decorated and busy; the scientist illustrations may carry likeness and rights issues; many type styles at once.

## Do NOT copy
Illustrations of real people (likeness), the exact purple-and-yellow brand pairing, the lantern/Arabic logo marks.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: single column, one highlight color, boxed rules; use original illustrated icons instead of portraits; keep equations centered with generous space. Black and white: highlights become 20 percent grey bars with bold labels.
