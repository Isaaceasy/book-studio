# KDP FACTS (defaults with dates; VERIFY LIVE before relying on any of them)

Rule: the live KDP help page wins. When a live page differs, update this file with the date. Never quote this file as proof; it is a starting point for faster, more accurate checks. Items marked (secondary) came from third-party pages and MUST be checked.

## Metadata
- Title plus subtitle together: fewer than 200 characters (KDP help G201097560, read 2026-10-29). Title must match the cover (print: front or spine).
- Prohibited in titles and subtitles: repeating generic keywords, unauthorized references to other titles, authors or trademarks, implied contributors, sales-rank words ("bestselling"), promotional words ("free"), placeholders, HTML tags.
- Seven backend keyword fields; description with allowed HTML only; no URLs, reviews, prices or promotional time-limits in the description. See `/kdp-metadata`.

## Content and AI disclosure (G200672390, read 2026-10-29)
- AI-generated text, images and translations must be disclosed when publishing; they remain AI-generated after substantial edits. AI-assisted (you wrote it, AI only edited or brainstormed) need not be disclosed.
- Content guidelines require a positive customer experience; misleading metadata or content that does not represent the book is not tolerated; content freely available on the web is not accepted unless the owner has the rights.
- Guide to Kindle Content Quality (G200952510): disappointing content includes books not significantly different from another book, too short, excessively reused content, copy-paste text, typos and formatting faults, blurry images.
- Low-content books (GGE5T76TWKA85DJM): nonfiction and textbooks are "Not Generally Low-Content".

## Paperback print
- Page count rules for 5 x 8 in (G201834180): standard color on white paper 72 to 600 pages; premium color 24 to 828; black ink 24 to 828 (white paper).
- Smallest US paperback trims: 5 x 8 and 5.06 x 7.81 in (trim sizes page, 2026-10-29). True pocket sizes are not offered.
- Printing cost, amazon.com, USD (G201834340, read 2026-10-29): small trim (up to 6.12 x 9 in): black ink 110 to 828 pp = 1.00 + 0.012 per page (24 to 110 pp = 2.30); standard color 72 to 600 pp = 1.00 + 0.0255 per page; premium color 42 to 828 pp = 1.00 + 0.065 per page. Large trim (over 6.12 in wide or 9 in tall): black 110 to 828 pp = 1.00 + 0.017 per page (24 to 110 pp = 2.84); standard color 72 to 600 pp = 1.00 + 0.0402 per page; premium color 42 to 828 pp = 1.00 + 0.08 per page.
- Royalty (G201834330): 60 percent at amazon.com list price of $9.99 or more (50 percent at or below $9.98); expanded distribution 40 percent; maximum list price $250 on amazon.com. Royalty = rate x list price minus printing cost.
- Margins (G201953870 / margins page, read 2026-10-29): inside margin minimum 0.375 in for 24 to 150 pages and 0.5 in for 151 to 300 pages; outside, top and bottom at least 0.25 in without bleed (0.375 in with bleed). If any page bleeds, set up the whole interior with bleed.

## Covers
- Paperback cover (KDP help G201953020, page read 2026-11-02): bleed 0.125 in on top, bottom and outside edges; cover width = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed; height = bleed + trim height + bleed. Spine width: black and white white paper = pages x 0.002252 in; cream = pages x 0.0025 in; Standard Color = pages x 0.002252 in; Premium Color = pages x 0.002347 in. Safe margins: front and back text at least 0.125 in inside the trim; spine text at least 0.0625 in from the spine edges; borders at least 0.25 in inside the trim. Spine text allowed only at 79 pages or more (Cover Creator requires 80); below that the cover is rejected if it has spine text. The cover must be ONE PDF with back, spine and front as one image, fonts embedded, transparencies flattened, no crop marks, color bars or security restrictions; images at least 300 dpi (the page says CMYK; many designers upload RGB and KDP converts: follow KDP's current uploader message); files over 650 MB will not convert (40 MB or less recommended). Barcode: the page gives no size; KDP places one if you supply none (lower-left for right-to-left books); take the zone from KDP's generated template (this framework reserves 2 x 1.2 in at the bottom right of the back cover as a clean zone). Odd interior page counts: not addressed on that page; the interior build notes say KDP adds a blank page, so compute the spine from the count KDP will print.
- Ebook cover (G200645690, search results 2026-11-02): ideal 2,560 px high by 1,600 px wide (ratio 1.6:1; 2,500 px high also suggested), minimum 1,000 x 625 px, maximum 10,000 px per side, JPEG or TIFF, RGB recommended (CMYK is converted), 50 MB maximum. Third-party pages often swap width and height or add a 300 dpi figure KDP does not state.
- Always use KDP's cover template or calculator for the exact trim, paper and ink, and KDP's online previewer.

## Other
- Always view the final interior and cover in KDP's online previewer and order a proof copy before approving.
- AI-generated cover or interior images: disclose; confirm the image tool's current commercial-use terms (HiggsField official terms read 2026-10-29: user owns outputs and may use them commercially; company may embed provenance metadata; no representing outputs as human-made; re-check at use).

## To verify before first use in a new book
Ebook royalty options and price limits; hardcover trims, page limits and royalty; A+ content module sizes for books; large-print recommendations; audiobook platform specs and AI-narration rules; any change to the 200-character limit, keyword limits, description limit and allowed HTML tags.
