## Typography
Arabic naskh/kufi-style sans for headings, humanist sans for body, about 9 to 10 pt (ESTIMATED). Closest open fonts: Noto Naskh Arabic / Cairo for RTL; Source Sans 3 / Nunito for Latin.

## Page furniture
Thin rounded frame around each page in orange or teal; ring-binding holes drawn on the gutter (mockup); small chevron page tabs; running ribbon with the unit title.

## Components
Rounded callout panels with a colored ribbon title; step circles; labelled cell diagrams; question-and-answer panels with tick markers; side images in rounded frames; highlighter-style emphasis on key terms.

## Strengths
Friendly, highly structured, easy to study from; consistent frame and ribbon system; mixes diagrams and short text well.

## Weaknesses
Busy; many colors and small text; spiral mockups hide the margins; RTL layouts need mirrored margins and tabs.

## Do NOT copy
The designer's monogram and the orange corner brackets, the ribbon shapes, the Arabic hashtag footer.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: one column, keep one frame style and ribbon, reduce to two accent colors; for LTR mirror the tab and ribbon logic. Black and white: ribbons in dark grey with white text, frames as 1 pt rules.
