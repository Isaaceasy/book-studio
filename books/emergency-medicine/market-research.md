## 4. Demand, competition, price band
**Demand signals.** Ten of the sampled titles rank under 60,000 overall (rows 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17), so the subject sells. The indie 6x9 title in row 2 reached #7,838 at $31.30, which shows a non-publisher book can rank well. Several 2026 indie books rank well in narrow categories (row 20 #220, row 21 #880) but this may reflect very thin category competition rather than volume.
**Competition signals.** Leaders by publisher are old or very large editions; row 4 and row 12 are 2026 editions. Review counts of the leaders are modest (90 to 523), so no title holds an unreachable review lead. Many new 2026 indie "clinical reference" books occupy the first search pages at $157 to $232 print; they compete for visibility but their price is far above the student band.
**Price band (observed, print).** Student-level and handbook titles: $16 to $60. Full textbooks: $100 to $222. Indie 2026 titles: $67 to $232. A realistic, defensible band for a 330-page-class student reference: about $30 to $50.
**Sales volume.** Not published by Amazon. Not estimated. Rank bands only: strong (below 50,000: rows 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17), moderate (50,000 to 300,000: rows 4, 10, 13, 14, 19, 22), weak (above 300,000: rows 3, 5, 6, 18).

## 5. Unit economics (KDP pages read 2026-10-28)
Rules: royalty 60% of list price at USD 9.99 or more (Amazon.com); maximum list price USD 250. Large trim (US Letter 8.5 x 11): black ink 110 to 828 pp = $1.00 + $0.017 per page; standard color 72 to 600 pp = $1.00 + $0.0402 per page; premium color 42 to 828 pp = $1.00 + $0.08 per page. Print cost varies by marketplace and options. KDP states a minimum list price exists (figure not shown on the page).

Page count is my estimate (about 132,000 words plus 50 figures and tables): 300, 350 or 400 pages. It depends on the final layout and must be rechecked at assembly.

Royalty = 0.60 x price minus print cost.

Premium color at 350 pp: 1.00 + 350 x 0.08 = $29.00 per copy; this loses money at every price tested except at $49.99 (royalty about +$1.00), so it is not viable.
Standard color is the owner's choice. It needs a list price of about $39.99 to $49.99 to earn roughly $7 to $15 per copy; at $29.99 it earns almost nothing. The observed student-textbook band reaches $47 to $55 (rows 1, 3), so $39.99 to $49.99 is inside it but at the upper end for an unknown author.

## 6. Verdict: CAUTION
- Demand for the subject is clearly shown: ten sampled titles rank under 60,000 overall and a $31 indie title reached #7,838.
- Competition is beatable on review count, but the shelf is crowded: established publisher editions plus a wave of 2026 indie titles.
- Economics are positive only at $39.99 or more with a color interior. At $29.99 color nets under $5 per copy, and a cheaper price band is where the best-ranking indie book sits.
- The book is wide (nursing + EMS + allied health, 12 Parts) and the first Amazon page shows no price point where this exact positioning is confirmed to sell. No review text was seen to confirm what readers dislike.
- Medical procedures raise policy and liability risk (section 8).
This is evidence-based judgment, not a prediction or guarantee of sales.

What would settle it: review themes on rows 1, 2, 3 and 11 (owner can paste them or share a signed-in view); a decision on color versus black and white; and a decision on price.

## 8. Policy and risk screen (first screen only)
- Medical content and step-by-step procedures: needs clinician clearance, safety callouts and disclaimers; no treatment-cure claims (checklist: medical claims).
- No trademarks or brand/author names in title, subtitle, description or keywords. Several leading titles carry protected series names; the subtitle must not use them.
- Look-alike titles: the first search pages hold near-identical 2026 titles with long keyword-heavy subtitles; avoid copying that style (metadata and misleading-title risk).
- AI disclosure: record exactly what is AI-generated or AI-assisted (text and images) for the KDP questions.
- Color interior and Letter size are large-trim and standard-color print-cost rules, so cost must be rechecked at the final page count.

## 9. Requirements handed to the outline
**Must address:** a mid-size connected-prose student reference; current-year standards; visual support at a price point of about $39.99 to $49.99; procedures with clinician-verified safety callouts; clear scope for each reader group.
**Must avoid:** brand/author/series names in metadata; dose figures; padding to reach the word floor; copying the competitor structure; any claim of outcomes, exam pass rates or rank.
**Reader level and price guidance:** first-year to upper-level student; target list price about $39.99 to $49.99 if color is kept.
