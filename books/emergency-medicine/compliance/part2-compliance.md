## Required fixes (REVISE), old text -> new text
1. Open-airway step 6. Old: "6. If the patient is breathing normally and not injured, and you must leave or cannot stay with them, place them on their side in the recovery position; otherwise keep them on the back with the airway held open." New: "6. If the patient is breathing normally and you do not suspect a neck or spine injury, place them in the recovery position and stay with them, checking their breathing; if you suspect a neck or spine injury, keep them on the back with the airway held open."
2. Open-airway Safety box (unsupported exception, see P1-076). Old: "do not move a patient with a possible neck injury unless the scene is unsafe or you must move them to give life-saving care, as your protocol directs." New: "do not move a patient with a possible neck injury unless the scene is unsafe or your protocol directs a move to give life-saving care, and then keep the head, neck and body in line as far as you can."
3. Choking procedure needs a stop-and-call line. Add after its Safety callout a stopline block: "**Stop and call for help if** the person cannot cough, speak or breathe, becomes unresponsive, or the object does not come out." Also step 1. Old: "1. Ask "Are you choking?" and check whether the person can cough, speak or breathe." New: "1. Ask "Are you choking?" and check whether the person can cough, speak or breathe. If they can cough, encourage them to keep coughing and stay close."
4. Recovery position needs scope and stop lines. Old (end of paragraph): "...and a patient with a possible spinal injury is moved only as your protocol directs." New: "...and a patient with a possible spinal injury is moved only as your protocol directs. This skill follows your training level and local protocol. Call for help if the patient's breathing becomes noisy, slow or stops."
5. Anaphylaxis Safety box. Old: "Do not give anything by mouth if the person has trouble breathing. Do not put a pillow under the head of a person who has trouble breathing. Do not assume earlier allergy shots give full protection. Used needles must go in a sharps container; keep your fingers away from the needle end of the device." New: "Do not give food or drink to a person who has trouble breathing. Used needles must go in a sharps container; keep your fingers away from the needle end of the device." (Restore the pillow sentence only if the fact-check opens a source and it agrees with "sitting up if breathing is hard".)
