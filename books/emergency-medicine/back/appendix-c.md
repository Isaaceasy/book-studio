# Appendix C: Reference Values {data-label="Reference" .back}

These tables are a reference copy of values given in the Parts. Normal values change with age, sex, weight, fitness and health, and your service may use different ranges. Follow your local protocol.

Table: Adult resting ranges (Entry 1.4)

| Measure | Typical range |
|:--------|:--------------|
| Pulse (heart rate) | 60 to 100 beats per minute |
| Breathing rate | 12 to 18 breaths per minute |
| Blood pressure | between 90/60 and 120/80 mm Hg |
| Temperature | 97.7 to 99.1 degrees F (36.5 to 37.3 degrees C) |

Table: Children's resting ranges, reference copy (Entry 6.1)

| Age | Breathing rate (breaths per minute) | Heart rate awake (beats per minute) |
|:----|:------------------------------------|:------------------------------------|
| Infant (1 month to 1 year) | 30 to 53 | 100 to 190 |
| Toddler (1 to 2 years) | 22 to 37 | 98 to 140 |
| Preschool (3 to 5 years) | 20 to 28 | 80 to 120 |
| School age (6 to 11 years) | 18 to 25 | 75 to 118 |
| Adolescent (12 to 15 years) | 12 to 20 | 60 to 100 |

Sources differ slightly, and newborns in the first month have their own ranges; follow the values in your protocol.

Table: Glasgow Coma Scale layout (Entry 1.3)

| Response | Score and description |
|:---------|:----------------------|
| Eyes | 4 opens spontaneously; 3 opens to sound; 2 opens to pressure; 1 none |
| Verbal | 5 oriented; 4 confused; 3 words only; 2 sounds only; 1 none |
| Motor | 6 obeys commands; 5 localizes pain; 4 normal flexion; 3 abnormal flexion; 2 extension; 1 none |

The three scores are added to give a total from 3 to 15. A score of 15 is the highest.

Table: Adult burn size by the rule of nines (Entry 4.5)

| Region | Share of body surface |
|:-------|:----------------------|
| Head and neck | 9 percent |
| Each arm | 9 percent |
| Front of trunk | 18 percent |
| Back of trunk | 18 percent |
| Each leg | 18 percent |
| Genital area | 1 percent |

The palm of the patient's hand, with the fingers, is about 1 percent of the body surface.
