# BOOK BIBLE: Emergency Nursing Fundamentals

Date 2026-10-29. Locked with the architecture after owner approval. New terms are added formally here before they are used in a chapter.

## 1. Reader profile
Nursing students, paramedic and EMT students, and allied-health students (for example respiratory therapy, radiography, surgical technology, pre-licensure health sciences) in the United States, in their first clinical placements or first emergency-care course. They have basic anatomy and physiology, have not yet managed emergencies, and need a short, trustworthy, visual guide to hold the order of priorities in their head. They are not practising independently; they act within a scope of practice and local protocol that the book repeatedly points them back to. Reading level: first-year college.

## 3. People, characters, places
No real people, patients, clinicians or institutions. Illustrative cases use first names only, original composites, no identifying details; settings generic (a school sports field, a gym, a camp, a trail, a home).

## 4. Facts register (verification-log ID per fact)

## 5. Running examples and recurring devices
- The primary survey order (ABCDE) is the book's spine and returns by plain-text bridge only.
- One memory aid per entry at most, labelled **Remember**.
- Recurring question to the reader: "Is this patient getting worse faster than I can help?" used at most once per Part.

## 6. Cross-reference list
Plain text only, for example "Entry 1.3 set out the primary survey." The list of bridges is in `outline.md` section 9.

## 7. Required disclaimers (front matter and where relevant)
1. Educational reference only; not a substitute for training, local protocol, medical direction or professional judgment.
2. In a medical emergency, contact local emergency services.
3. Practice varies by state, agency and level of training; follow your own scope of practice and protocols.
4. No medicine doses are given (verified emergency-procedure figures such as compression rate are); drug names appear for recognition only.
5. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any professional association or certifying organization.
6. AI statement: wording must be accurate and match the KDP disclosure answers recorded in `status.md` (text, images and captions are AI-generated; do not describe as merely "assisted"). Also state that the book has not been validated for any individual state protocol.
7. Fictional cases are illustrative, not real patients.

## 8. Image style (filled during writing)
All anatomical images and illustrations: HiggsField, one consistent style (flat vector-like medical illustration, soft shading, neutral skin-tone range, simple backgrounds, crimson accent from the design system, no text inside the image, no realistic photographs, no real people). Labels, arrows and captions are added in the layout, not generated. SVG for flowcharts, algorithms and schematics in the same palette. Every figure inspected at 5 x 8 in for legibility before acceptance; every anatomical figure queued for clinician review. Final style prompt and seed notes recorded at the first figure.
