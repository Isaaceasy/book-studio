# PROFILE: FICTION (novels, novellas, story collections)

All standing rules apply except the nonfiction-only ones (fact-check ledger for factual claims inside the story, non-repetition of concepts, paragraph-based exposition). Default output: KDP PDF (black and white interior, usually 5.5 x 8.5 or 6 x 9) plus ebook.

## What is different
- **No citations and no bibliography** (nothing to cite).
- **Originality and rights:** no copying or lightly paraphrasing existing stories; no real people; no imitation of a living author's distinctive style; no trademarked characters, brands or settings; quote no lyrics or poems.
- **Continuity bible** replaces the terminology table: characters (age, appearance, voice, arc), timeline, places and maps (text only), world rules, recurring objects, facts already revealed, open threads. Updated after every chapter; the audit checks every chapter against it.
- **Structure lock:** logline, premise, themes, POV and tense, chapter-by-chapter beat sheet with the purpose of each chapter, word targets, and the ending; changes need the owner's approval.
- **Voice:** a voice sheet with 3 sample paragraphs; dialogue tags and idiom rules; banned clichés list; scene-over-summary targets.

## Audits (per chapter)
(C) continuity against the bible; (V) voice consistency; (P) pacing and scene purpose (every scene changes something); (D) dialogue naturalness and distinct voices; (S) sensitivity and content flags (violence, sexual content, self-harm, hate, minors, real places and institutions) with the owner's content decisions recorded; (R) repetition of images, phrases and scene beats; (A) AI-style tells (same sentence openers, tidy moral endings, stacked triplets, "tapestry" vocabulary).
Amazon: adult content must be marked correctly in KDP; content involving minors has strict limits; check the live content guidelines for the genre.

## CMS for fiction
Study genre conventions on the live bestseller subcategories: cover conventions, trope promises in descriptions, series patterns, word-count and price bands, ebook versus paperback rank behaviour (Kindle Unlimited signals are visible only partly; never claim sales). The model blueprint is about promise and package, never plot copying.

## Interior and cover
Interior: simple, readable, widow and orphan control, scene-break ornaments, running heads, drop cap openers; spine and cover per `/cover-design` with genre-specific concepts (typography and imagery that read as the genre at thumbnail size); ebook cover required.

## Length
Set in the Scope Lock by genre norm (for example 60,000 to 100,000 words for most novels); never pad.
