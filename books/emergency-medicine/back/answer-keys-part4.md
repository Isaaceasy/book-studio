8. Model answer: (1) Make the scene safe and call for help early, because confusion with a hot body in the heat suggests heat stroke, which is an emergency. (2) Move him to a cool, shaded place, remove excess clothing and check his airway and breathing. (3) Cool his body quickly by the method your protocol directs, such as cool water or wet cloths with fanning, and keep watching his airway and responsiveness. (4) Give nothing to drink while he is confused, record the times and his responsiveness, and stop cooling when he is cool enough as your protocol directs. This is an emergency because the body temperature has risen to a dangerous level and the brain is affected.
