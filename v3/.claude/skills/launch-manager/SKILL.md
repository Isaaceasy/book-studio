# /launch-manager: THE 30-DAY LAUNCH PLAN

## Purpose
GENIE Max "30-day launch manager: structured plan with timed checkpoints, preparation tasks, release activities and post-launch follow-through." It turns the finished package into a calendar the owner can follow.

## Inputs
`release/` (files and metadata), `final/KDP-upload-guide.md`, `cms-research.md` (price band, competitors), `book.yaml`, the owner's planned publish date and marketplaces, their audience channels (email list, social, communities) and budget.

## Procedure
1. **Ask once** (one question block): planned publish date, marketplaces, formats live at launch (ebook, paperback, hardcover, audio), channels the owner already controls, ad budget, and whether a pre-order or free-promotion is wanted (verify live whether the options exist for the format).
2. **Build the calendar** `launch/launch-plan.md` with day offsets (T-30 to T+30):
   - **T-30 to T-14 preparation:** final proof copy ordered and checked in KDP's online previewer, metadata final (title, subtitle, description, 7 keywords, categories), cover approved, A+ content ready, author page ready (`/author-brand`), reader-magnet ready, launch email drafted, landing page ready, ads plan ready (`/ads-manager`), review-policy reminders.
   - **T-13 to T-1:** upload steps from the guide, publish date set, series and edition linking planned, pricing confirmed, notification of existing readers (own list only), schedule of posts the owner writes and sends.
   - **T0 release day:** publish confirmation check, listing check (title, cover, description, A+ appears, categories, price), start of low-bid auto campaigns, announcement to the owner's own audience, first review-readiness reminder (never ask for positive reviews or offer incentives).
   - **T+1 to T+14:** daily checks of listing health and rank movement (observed, not promised), ad review at day 3 and day 7, answer customer questions the owner receives, correct any listing error.
   - **T+15 to T+30:** first weekly optimization from real data, decision on price and keywords, plan for editions or the next book, retro for `docs/lessons-learned.md`.
3. **Checkpoint table:** date offset, task, owner or Claude-prepared, files needed, done flag, and the policy reminder that applies.
4. **Prepared assets only:** draft emails, post copy and a launch announcement based on the approved description; no claims beyond the manuscript; no fake urgency; no prices or rank claims; no requests for reviews beyond what Amazon permits.
5. **Update** `status.md` and stop. The owner marks tasks done; later sessions can read `launch/launch-plan.md` and continue.

## Rules
- Never contact anyone, post, email, buy, or change any account setting. Drafts only.
- Never include review solicitation tricks, incentives, review swaps or fake engagement. Reviews come from real readers.
