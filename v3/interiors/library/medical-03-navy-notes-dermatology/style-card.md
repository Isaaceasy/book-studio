# STYLE CARD: medical-03-navy-notes-dermatology

- **Source:** `medicine book interior 3.pdf` (8 pages, dermatology lecture-notes style, bilingual labels visible). `source: third-party`
- **Analysed:** 2026-11-02 by Claude from the spread on PDF page 3 (viewed). Other pages not individually reviewed.
- **Book type and niche it suits:** illustrated lecture notes, photo-rich clinical atlases, student revision notes. Reader: students. Tone: practical, note-like.
- **Page size and trim:** ESTIMATED about 8 x 10.5 in.
- **Color mode:** color; **density:** balanced, photo-led, bold-keyword emphasis.

## Palette (ESTIMATED)
| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | #1F3A5F | chapter tab, page-number box, footer |
| Secondary | #8FA3C7 | topic label bars (light blue-grey gradient) |
| Accent | #4A7C2F and #7A7F8E | key-term colour words (green, grey) |
| Tints | #EEF1F7 | page tint |
Greyscale behaviour: label bars are mid-grey with dark text: fine; key-term colour words lose meaning, so they also need bold.

## Typography
Body: neutral humanist sans, about 9 pt, bold for key terms and coloured emphasis for defined terms; sub-topic labels on shaded bars with a small star bullet; captions beneath photos in blue bold ("Figure09: ..."). Closest open fonts: Roboto, Open Sans.

## Page furniture
Top-left chapter tab (navy) next to the subject name in brackets; footer line with author and institution and a navy page-number block; photo columns right-aligned with text wrap.

## Components
Topic bars (shaded rectangles with a bullet), bullet lists with mixed bold, "Clinical types / Differential diagnosis" mini-headings, photo plates with numbered captions, colour-coded keywords.

## Strengths
Photo-and-text integration; label bars make topics instantly findable; consistent figure captioning.

## Weaknesses
Mixed languages and colour-coded words reduce consistency; gradients on bars are heavy; photo-dependent (needs real clinical photos, which our rules do not allow without consent: use illustrations).

## Do NOT copy
The exact chapter-tab plus bracketed-subject header, the star-bullet label bars and the institution footer.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: single column, topic bars become full-width tinted headings, figures at column width; replace photo plates with illustrations (our privacy rule: no real clinical photographs). Black and white: bars become 15 percent grey with bold dark text.

