# PROFILE: COOKBOOKS, CRAFTS, TRAVEL AND HOBBY GUIDES (image-heavy lifestyle nonfiction)

All standing rules apply. Default output: KDP PDF in color (standard color; check print cost at the page count), often 8 x 10 in or 8.5 x 8.5 in for image-led books.

## Structure
Cookbook: introduction (equipment, pantry, safety), chapters by meal or technique, each recipe in a fixed template: title, yield, time (prep, cook, total), ingredients (metric and US units as the region requires), method as numbered steps, notes (substitutions, storage, allergens), optional nutrition (only if calculated and labelled estimated). Craft or hobby: materials list, steps, troubleshooting. Travel: practical, dated, region-specific information with currency dates.

## Safety and accuracy audit
- (M) Measurements and conversions recomputed by script, both unit systems consistent.
- (F) Food safety: temperatures, times, storage and allergen statements checked against current official food-safety sources opened and logged; allergens flagged in every recipe.
- (N) Nutrition figures: only if computed from a stated method; label as estimates; no health claims.
- (R) Recipe testing statement: never claim recipes were tested unless the owner confirms they were; otherwise say so honestly in the front matter or do not claim.
- (P) Prices, opening hours and addresses for travel content: verify with dates; add "check before you go".

## Images
HiggsField food and scene images must not look like real photographs of a specific restaurant or brand; no logos, no packaging brands; check each image against its recipe (ingredients visible, plausible portions); disclose AI-generated images; a Figure Log is kept. Alt text for ebooks.

## Interior and cover
Library cards for lifestyle books; ink and paper cost matters (color interior, many images): compute the royalty at the real page count before locking the product spec. Cover: appetizing, high contrast, thumbnail test.

## Length
Set in the Scope Lock (recipe count and pages). No near-duplicate recipes; every recipe must be complete and doable.
