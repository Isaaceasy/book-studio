# STYLE CARD: medical-01-crimson-charcoal-guidelines

- **Source:** `medicine book interior 1.pdf` (12 pages, 8.5 x 11 in mockup sheets). A designer credit is visible on the mockup (internal only). `source: third-party`
- **Analysed:** 2026-11-02 by Claude from pages 2, 3 (spread images viewed). Other pages not individually reviewed.
- **Book type and niche it suits:** clinical guideline summaries, quick-reference medicine, study guides. Reader: students and practising clinicians. Tone: crisp, authoritative, high-energy.
- **Page size and trim:** ESTIMATED about 7 x 10 in per page in the mockup (spread shown); PDF sheet 8.5 x 11.
- **Color mode:** color; **density:** balanced to dense (about 350 to 450 words per page, ESTIMATED), heavy use of tables.

## Palette (ESTIMATED from the mockup)
| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | #C8102E | header diamonds, table header row, banner pills, footer band |
| Secondary | #3A3A3C | charcoal chevron bars, footer centre tab, rules |
| Accent | #E8E8EA | page background tint |
| Tints | #FBE9EC | soft rows and highlights |
Greyscale behaviour: crimson and charcoal separate well in greyscale; banners rely on white text on mid-dark fill.

## Typography
Body: clean humanist or neo-grotesque sans, about 9 to 9.5 pt, leading about 1.4, numbered recommendations with hanging numerals. Headings: rounded geometric sans in crimson for the running title. Banner pill: bold white sans on a crimson dashed-outline pill. Closest open fonts: Nunito or Quicksand (headings), Inter or Source Sans (body).

## Page furniture
Running header: topic title in crimson with a charcoal double-bar and a centred crimson diamond ornament; footer: crimson band with a charcoal trapezoid tab holding the page number; generous outer margin; two-line header rules.

## Components
Numbered lists for principles and recommendations; section labels as small crimson-outlined tags with a hatched end ("GUIDING PRINCIPLES", "RECOMMENDATIONS"); dashed-border banner pill for the source or update title; rounded-corner table with a crimson header row, thin dark grid, symbols (+, ++, minus) with a legend beneath.

## Strengths
Instantly scannable; strong color identity; tables are the hero; legend lines make color-coded meaning explicit; footer and header frame each page.

## Weaknesses
Dense; decoration (diamonds, chevrons) can feel busy; small body size; low contrast between charcoal and crimson in places.

## Do NOT copy
The exact diamond-and-chevron header, the trapezoid page-number tab, the dashed banner pill treatment and the specific crimson plus charcoal pairing as a brand signature.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: drop the double-bar header to a single thin rule; keep one tag style for labels; make tables one column wide with 8.5 pt text; pair crimson with a warm sand or slate instead of charcoal to stay distinct. Black-and-white edition: header rows in black with white text; replace +/- symbols with words.
