Part 1 answer key (goes to Appendix A at assembly)

1. B. Scene safety comes before patient contact because an injured responder becomes a second patient.
2. C. Triage orders patients by how soon they must be seen to stay safe, not by arrival, volume of complaint or expected stay.
3. B. A level is a starting point; a patient who deteriorates is reassessed, moved to a higher level and escalated.
4. C. The order is Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure.
5. B. A patient who responds only to a loud voice is rated V (voice).
6. C. A pulse oximeter does not show how well a patient is breathing out carbon dioxide, so a normal reading can coexist with poor ventilation.
7. C. Findings in survey order, the vital signs and what you did belong in Assessment.
8. Model answer: (1) Check the scene for hazards and protect yourself. (2) Take the first look and assess responsiveness. (3) Call for help early, because fast breathing and a reduced response to voice suggest the patient may worsen. (4) Assess airway and breathing, count and record the breathing rate, then continue through circulation, disability and exposure and treat problems as found.
