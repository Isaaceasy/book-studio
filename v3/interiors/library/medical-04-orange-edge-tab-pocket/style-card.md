# STYLE CARD: medical-04-orange-edge-tab-pocket

- **Source:** `Pocket_Neurology_photo_inside_1500x.webp`, a publisher's product photo of a pocket-size clinical series spread (also shows the series cover system). `source: third-party`
- **Analysed:** 2026-11-02 by Claude from the single photo.
- **Book type and niche it suits:** compact clinical pocket references, quick-learning guides, revision series. Reader: students and junior clinicians. Tone: fast, visual.
- **Page size and trim:** pocket paperback, ESTIMATED about 4.5 x 6.5 in (smaller than any KDP trim; on KDP the nearest is 5 x 8).
- **Color mode:** color; **density:** dense text beside large labelled illustrations.

## Palette (ESTIMATED)
| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | #E8731A | thick orange edge tab on both outer edges |
| Secondary | #F4A261 | sub-head underline, callout fill |
| Accent | #6C5FA8 | illustration shading (purple/blue regions) |
| Tints | #FBE3D1 | "pearl" callout box fill |
Greyscale behaviour: orange tab becomes a dark mid-grey bar; callouts need a border.

## Typography
Body: compact humanist sans about 8 pt; sub-heads orange with a thin rule; condition names large; figure labels small with leader lines; section name rotated on the tab ("CONDITIONS") with the page number in a white notch. Closest open fonts: Source Sans 3, Lato.

## Page furniture
Full-height coloured edge tabs (outer edge) carrying page number and section label; thin inner margin; illustrations spanning the full column width.

## Components
Large labelled medical illustrations with leader lines and figure captions ("Figure 84 // ..."); icon callouts (lightbulb "tip" box on a tinted fill); short bulleted facts after a heading; consistent series colour-coding on covers.

## Strengths
The edge tab gives instant navigation; illustrations are first-class; tip boxes break dense text; the series colour system supports a catalog look.

## Weaknesses
Very small type; leader-line labels need high-resolution art; tabs consume the outer margin.

## Do NOT copy
The exact tab shape with the page-number notch, the series cover system and the lightbulb tip icon.

## Adaptation notes
Closest KDP trim is 5 x 8 in; the full-height tab needs bleed (then the whole interior must be set up with bleed) or an inset tab (the emergency nursing book used an inset tab 0.27 in from the trim, no bleed). Keep type at 9 to 9.5 pt for legibility. Black and white: tab in 80 percent black, tip box with a border.
