## Typography
Bold geometric sans headings, light sans body about 9 pt; large numerals; short captions. Closest open fonts: Poppins, Montserrat, Lato.

## Page furniture
Modular grid with photo tiles; thin top rules; pull-out statistic blocks; product sheets with a colored left bar and key facts in a column.

## Components
Photo grids, infographic icons, data callouts, mission and values pages, product feature rows.

## Strengths
Clean modular grid; a good source of layout ideas for lead magnets, reader bonuses and A+ content pages.

## Weaknesses
Photo-dependent; not designed for long text.

## Do NOT copy
Brand identities and photographs.

## Adaptation notes
Use for reader magnets, one-page bonus PDFs and A+ images rather than book interiors; photos replaced by generated illustrations or owned images.
