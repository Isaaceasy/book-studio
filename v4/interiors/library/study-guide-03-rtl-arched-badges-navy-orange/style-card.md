## Typography
Arabic display for unit titles inside arched shapes; clean sans for body about 9 pt. Closest open fonts: Cairo, Almarai, Noto Sans Arabic.

## Page furniture
Right page unit opener: a tall arched or lantern-shaped badge with a line icon and the unit title below; running footers with a small label; bottom stripe band in navy lines on the mockup background.

## Components
Arched badge section markers; hexagon or ribbon-shaped question tiles; 'tick box' rows; toggle-like true/false switches; ruled answer lines; concept boxes in rounded ribbons.

## Strengths
Memorable unit openers, clear navigation, good rhythm of airy opener plus structured activity pages.

## Weaknesses
Arched motif strongly signals one cultural style; many small tiles reduce readability at small sizes.

## Do NOT copy
The arched/lantern badge silhouette and its exact ornament, the toggle switch graphics.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: use a simple shaped badge opener scaled to the top third and tiles reduced to a single column; for non-RTL books use a neutral geometry (circle, rounded square).
