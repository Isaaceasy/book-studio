## Typography
Condensed or semi-bold sans chapter titles in a solid block, serif or clean sans body about 10.5 pt. Closest open fonts: Montserrat/Oswald (titles), Source Serif 4 (body).

## Page furniture
Left page: full-height navy block with chapter number and title in white; right page: text with a drop quote; page footer line with a small red rule; credit guide: teal rectangle title bar.

## Components
Chapter opener blocks, bold keyword lead-ins, callouts with teal bars, bullet lists with colored markers, pull quotes.

## Strengths
Easy to read, professional; the chapter block gives instant identity; low ink cost on inner pages.

## Weaknesses
Standard; relies on the opener to differentiate.

## Do NOT copy
The exact opener block proportions and the red foot rule combined with navy.

## Adaptation notes
Good for 5 x 8 in self-help and business: reduce the opener to the top third, keep a single callout style, add a unique accent and a distinctive numeral treatment.
