# STYLE CARD: medical-02-purple-teal-serif-textbook

- **Source:** `medicine book interior 2.pdf` (10 pages) and one webp spread (`231fe430...webp`) showing a chapter opener and text spread. `source: third-party`
- **Analysed:** 2026-11-02 by Claude from the contents and acknowledgements spread (PDF page 3) and the webp chapter spread.
- **Book type and niche it suits:** traditional medical textbooks (obstetrics style), clinical science chapters with case scenarios. Reader: medical, nursing and allied-health students. Tone: scholarly, calm.
- **Page size and trim:** ESTIMATED about 7.5 x 9.5 in (large perfect-bound textbook).
- **Color mode:** color (two-colour accent system); **density:** dense body text in two columns on text pages, airy openers.

## Palette (ESTIMATED)
| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | #7B4A9C | headings, chapter numeral, sub-heads |
| Secondary | #1A8FA6 | thin vertical rule at chapter openers, figure labels |
| Accent | #C0A3D6 | box header fills |
| Tints | #EFEAD6 | sidebar box body (warm cream) |
Greyscale behaviour: purple headings become mid-grey and still separate from black body; teal rule is light and disappears (use a darker teal or a thicker rule).

## Typography
Body: classical serif (Century or Sabon style), about 9.5 to 10 pt, leading about 1.35, justified. Chapter title: large light-weight sans-serif italic with a bold chapter numeral in purple. Sub-heads: bold italic sans in purple. Captions: sans, teal figure label. Closest open fonts: Source Serif 4 or Crimson Pro (body), Inter or Hanken Grotesk (headings).

## Page furniture
Running head in small sans with page number outside; chapter opener with a thin vertical teal rule beside a very large title and the numeral; "Case scenario" in italic serif with a small purple label; table of contents with purple section labels and tinted entry numbers.

## Components
Case scenario at the chapter start; Introduction block; bullet lists with square bullets; box with a purple header bar and cream body for a key topic list; figure with leader-line labels and a teal "Figure 1.1" caption label.

## Strengths
Elegant hierarchy with only two accent colors; classic readability; easy to scale to long chapters; the chapter opener is memorable and calm.

## Weaknesses
Two-column body is too narrow on small trims; light-weight italic title can be hard in greyscale; tint-heavy boxes cost ink on color print.

## Do NOT copy
The exact opener composition (vertical rule plus oversized italic title plus numeral) and the specific purple-and-teal pairing as a publisher signature.

## Adaptation notes
5 x 8 in: single column, 10 pt body with 1.4 leading, keep the case-scenario device but as a boxed "Illustrative case"; reduce the opener to a shorter title with a numeral and rule. Black and white: use bold weights and rules for hierarchy.
