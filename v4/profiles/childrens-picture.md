# PROFILE: CHILDREN'S PICTURE BOOKS AND EARLY READERS

All standing rules apply. Default output: KDP PDF in color (standard or premium color), square or landscape trim (for example 8.5 x 8.5 or 11 x 8.5), usually 24 to 40 pages for picture books; early readers longer. Amazon is strict about children's content: read the live content guidelines for children's books and metadata.

## Structure
Age band and reading level locked first (for example 3 to 5, 4 to 7). Story as a page-by-page storyboard: page number, text (2 to 4 short lines or per the age band), illustration brief, mood, page-turn hook. A repeating motif or refrain, a clear problem and warm resolution, a gentle takeaway without a lecture. Rhyme only if the meter is checked line by line.

## Safety and fit audits
- (A) Age fit: vocabulary, concepts and fears suitable for the age band.
- (K) Child safety: no dangerous imitation (for example unsafe play, unsafe products), no frightening content beyond the band, no stereotypes, inclusive and respectful.
- (T) Trademarks and characters: no existing characters, brands or songs; characters original.
- (I) Illustration consistency: each character looks the same on every page (a character sheet), correct hands and faces, no stray text in images, no scary distortions.
- (R) Read-aloud test: the text is read aloud for rhythm and length.

## Illustration pipeline
HiggsField (or the owner's illustrator): create a character sheet and a style sheet first; generate each page with the same references and seed notes where available; keep a Figure Log; reject inconsistent pages; text is added by script so there is no AI lettering; leave safe zones for the text; check bleed and the gutter. Disclose AI-generated illustrations to KDP.

## Interior and cover
Landscape or square layout with generous margins and large readable type; text never in the gutter; library cards for children's books in `interiors/`; cover conventions from the CMS (bright, simple, title legible small); ebook version as fixed layout or image-based pages (verify KDP's live children's ebook requirements).

## Length and price
Page count by KDP rules (color picture books are costly per page: compute royalty before locking the spec).
